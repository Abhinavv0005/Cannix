Cookies

What Are Cookies?

A cookie is a very small text document that often includes an anonymous unique identifier. When you visit a website, that site’s computer asks your computer for permission to store a cookie in a part of your hard drive specifically designated for such files. Each website can send its own cookie to your browser if your browser’s preferences allow it, but to protect your privacy your browser only permits a Web site to access the cookies it has already sent to you, not the cookies sent to you by other sites.
