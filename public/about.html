The Legacy of Cannabis and Hemp

The story of cannabis and hemp is as old as civilization itself. Revered for its incredible versatility, hemp has been used for over 10,000 years, making it one of humanity's oldest cultivated plants. In India, references to cannabis appear in ancient texts like the Rigveda, where it is described as a sacred plant, and in Ayurveda, where it is praised for its healing properties.

Hemp has served countless purposes throughout history:

  • Fabric and Rope: Used by ancient civilizations to create durable textiles and ropes.
  • Food Source: Rich in nutrients, hemp seeds were consumed for sustenance.
  • Medicinal Benefits: Documented in ancient Chinese, Egyptian, and Indian texts for treating pain, inflammation, and other ailments.

Today, hemp is recognized as one of the most sustainable plants on the planet. It requires minimal water, enriches the soil, and absorbs more carbon dioxide than most other plants, making it a crucial ally in combating climate change.

India’s Evolving Relationship with Hemp

In a historic move, the Indian government and the Ministry of AYUSH are progressively acknowledging the medicinal value of hemp. Recently, cannabis was reclassified from a Schedule 1 drug under NDPS to a prescription-based treatment, paving the way for its responsible use in wellness. This marks a significant step in rediscovering hemp's potential, blending ancient traditions with modern health solutions.

Understanding CBD and THC

CBD (Cannabidiol) and THC (Tetrahydrocannabinol) are two primary compounds found in cannabis. Together, they interact with the body’s endocannabinoid system, which regulates mood, sleep, pain, and immunity.

  • CBD: Non-psychoactive, known for reducing anxiety, promoting restful sleep, and relieving pain and inflammation.
  • THC: Offers pain relief, appetite stimulation, and relaxation, when used responsibly.
